# Global Equestrian E-commerce & Services Landscape

A scan of leading and notable platforms across tack retail, horse trading, services directories, auctions, subscriptions, content-commerce hybrids, booking platforms, and the India/Asia/Middle East region. Compiled June 2026 for the Indusequine founder as a feature-mining exercise.

---

## 1. Tack & equestrian retail

### SmartPak / SmartEquine (USA)
- **What it does:** Direct retailer of horse supplements, tack, blankets and rider apparel; pioneered pre-measured daily supplement packs.
- **Business model:** Direct retail + AutoShip subscription. Rebranded SmartPak → SmartEquine in July 2025; acquired by Chewy in late 2025.
- **Standout:** "SmartPaks" — one-click custom-built daily supplement regimens, pre-measured per horse, shipped monthly. Free SmartPerks loyalty layer (10% off in-house brands when AutoShip is active). 100% Happiness Guarantee on supplements.
- **Region:** USA.

### Dover Saddlery (USA)
- **What it does:** Multi-channel English-discipline tack and apparel retailer (10,000+ SKUs) with a strong omnichannel footprint and printed catalog heritage.
- **Business model:** Direct retail (own + 3rd-party brands), retail stores, catalog and e-com.
- **Standout:** "Blanket Wizard" guided fit tool, monogramming/engraving as a checkout add-on, in-house catalog requests still drive significant traffic, store-locator first-class citizen.
- **Region:** USA (Wellesley MA HQ, 30+ stores).

### Horze (Scandinavia → Global)
- **What it does:** Vertically integrated own-brand riding apparel and horse equipment retailer.
- **Business model:** D2C own-brand + multi-brand wholesale, 14 owned stores, online in 12 countries, 11 languages.
- **Standout:** Heavy own-brand share (high margin), Shopify replatform in 2026 with poq-powered iOS/Android app, multilingual storefront infrastructure, "large inventory + simple UX + fast delivery" as the explicit operating brief.
- **Region:** EU HQ Fulda; sells US, AU, EU, Scandinavia.

### Riding Warehouse (USA)
- **What it does:** Multi-brand specialty retailer covering English, Western, and Endurance.
- **Business model:** Direct retail; free shipping + free returns above $75.
- **Standout:** Riding Warehouse Learning Center (deep editorial / product education hub), pro-rider product reviews, live chat staffed by riders.
- **Region:** USA (San Luis Obispo CA).

### Krämer Pferdesport / Kramer Equestrian (Germany / EU)
- **What it does:** 25,000+ SKUs across 100+ brands; the European market leader in equestrian retail.
- **Business model:** Catalog + e-com + ~30 megastores across DE/AT/FR. 30-day money-back guarantee.
- **Standout:** Megastore + catalog + e-com hybrid that mirrors what Decathlon does in mass sports — scale of physical experience as competitive moat. Dedicated iOS app.
- **Region:** Germany, Austria, France, UK shop.

### Epplejeck (Benelux)
- **What it does:** Largest Benelux equestrian retailer; 16 superstores plus a Europe-wide web shop.
- **Business model:** Omnichannel, sales kiosks in-store integrated with e-com, PE-backed (Mentha) for European rollout.
- **Standout:** In-store sales kiosks that hand off to e-com inventory — solves the long-tail SKU problem for brick-and-mortar.
- **Region:** Netherlands, Belgium, Germany.

### Decathlon / Fouganza (France → India)
- **What it does:** Mass-market entry-level horse riding range — helmets, boots, gloves, saddles, bridles, fly masks.
- **Business model:** Own-brand vertical retail through Decathlon stores + decathlon.in + Flipkart marketplace.
- **Standout:** Already present in India at sharp price points; the de facto entry-level brand for first-time Indian riders. Anyone premium-positioned in India will compete *above* Fouganza, not against it.
- **Region:** Global, including India.

## 2. Horse buying/selling marketplaces

### ehorses.com (Germany / EU)
- **What it does:** Europe's leading horse marketplace, 17,000+ active listings, 300+ new horses daily.
- **Business model:** Paid listings + premium placement.
- **Standout:** A dedicated **"vet-check incl. x-rays available"** filter — 2,100+ listings carry pre-uploaded vet certificates and x-rays. Discipline + breed filters are extremely granular (e.g. "German Sport Horse" vs "Spanish Sport Horse" as separate buckets). Multi-language EU-wide reach.
- **Region:** Germany-led, pan-Europe.

### HorseClicks (USA)
- **What it does:** Mass-market US classifieds covering horses, trailers, tack, equestrian property.
- **Business model:** Free + paid listings; **PRO Business** subscription at ~$20/mo with declining cost-per-ad as volume grows; boost credits to lift ads in search.
- **Standout:** Seller dashboard with week-over-week analytics, "boost credits" mechanic, business tier explicitly for dealers (Indusequine could mirror this for Indian breeders/dealers).
- **Region:** USA.

### DreamHorse.com (USA)
- **What it does:** Long-running classifieds across sale, lease, trade, adoption, auction, stallions.
- **Business model:** Free basic ads, paid photo ads (only photo ads get hit-counters + seller home page link).
- **Standout:** Multi-disposition listings (lease/trade/adoption, not just sale) — useful for the Indian market where leasing/half-leasing is a wedge into ownership.
- **Region:** USA.

### Sport Horse Nation (USA)
- **What it does:** Discipline-specific classifieds (eventing + a newer Dressage Only spinoff).
- **Business model:** Paid listings, tightly editorial.
- **Standout:** Discipline-vertical sub-sites — a model for spinning up India-specific verticals (polo, tent-pegging, endurance, racing-retiree rehoming).
- **Region:** USA.

### HorseQuest (UK)
- **What it does:** Dominant UK classifieds with 700,000+ monthly visits.
- **Business model:** Paid listings; **official classifieds partner of British Eventing and British Dressage** — federation-backed traffic and trust.
- **Standout:** The federation-partnership model is directly replicable with the Equestrian Federation of India (EFI), Indian Polo Association, Amateur Riders' Club, etc.
- **Region:** UK / Ireland.

### HorseDeals (Australia)
- **What it does:** Australia's main classifieds platform.
- **Business model:** Listings free under AUD $5,000, paid above — a clever floor that lowers friction for entry-level horse sales.
- **Standout:** Price-banded listing fees — democratises the lower end while monetising premium listings.
- **Region:** Australia.

### Equirodi (France / Belgium / UK / CH)
- **What it does:** Classifieds for horses + ponies + livery yards + horseboxes + properties + jobs + equipment.
- **Business model:** Free photo+video listings; layered Equirodi Shopping marketplace for used-tack circular economy.
- **Standout:** **Circular-economy pitch** — explicit "give a second life to riding gear" positioning. Equestrian directory inside the same product. Free video upload at the listing level.
- **Region:** France, Belgium, Switzerland, UK.

### Pferd.de (Germany)
- **What it does:** German-language equine classifieds + community.
- **Business model:** Paid listings, ads.
- **Standout:** Long-running German community presence; useful as a positioning reference for community + classifieds bundling.
- **Region:** Germany.

## 3. Used tack / second-hand equestrian

### TackTrader.com (USA, part of DreamHorse)
- **What it does:** Used and new saddles, tack, show clothes, trailers.
- **Business model:** Paid classifieds, shared infra with DreamHorse.
- **Standout:** Riding the same plumbing as a horse classifieds site — cross-sell at zero marginal cost.
- **Region:** USA.

### TackTack (Global, mobile-first)
- **What it does:** Buy/sell used horse tack and apparel app.
- **Business model:** Marketplace, app store distribution (iOS).
- **Standout:** Mobile-native, Depop-style UX for tack — a model for Gen Z riders. Indian riders are mobile-first; this is the right reference.
- **Region:** Global (US-led).

### Equine Exchange / Northwest Tack Consignment (USA)
- **What it does:** Consignment model — physical shop receives used tack, sells on commission.
- **Business model:** Consignment commission (~30–40%).
- **Standout:** Hybrid physical-receive + online-sell model — could work in Bangalore/Delhi/Mumbai where authentication of used saddles matters.
- **Region:** USA regional.

### eBay / Facebook Marketplace / Etsy (Global)
- **What it does:** Generalist marketplaces with strong long-tail equestrian sub-categories.
- **Business model:** Marketplace fees / commission / free with ads.
- **Standout:** They cover the bottom of the market by default. A specialist platform wins on trust, authentication, and discipline-specific filters they don't have.

## 4. Services directories (coaches, vets, farriers, yards, transport)

### Yard and Groom (Global, UK-origin)
- **What it does:** World's leading equestrian jobs / recruitment board since 2002 — grooms, riders, instructors, yard managers.
- **Business model:** Paid employer listings + featured ads.
- **Standout:** Role-granular taxonomy (competition groom, dressage groom, show groom, head groom). Directly portable to India where the groom/syce labour market is informal and undigitised.
- **Region:** Global.

### British Horse Society (BHS) Approved Centres directory (UK)
- **What it does:** Searchable map of BHS-approved riding schools, livery yards, instructors.
- **Business model:** Membership + accreditation fees.
- **Standout:** **Accreditation as a feature.** The directory's value is BHS verification — sites are "approved," not just listed. EFI-approved schools/coaches/farriers could be the Indian equivalent.
- **Region:** UK.

### Hopoti (Finland → EU)
- **What it does:** Two-sided platform connecting riders with stables — lesson booking + stable management.
- **Business model:** SaaS for stables + consumer marketplace.
- **Standout:** Partnered with Equestrian Federation of Finland; integrated stable management software so that bookings flow into the same tool yards already use for invoicing and rota.
- **Region:** Finland, expanding EU.

### Roverd / Anolla / Equclub / SuperSaaS (Global)
- **What it does:** Booking SaaS for trail rides, lessons, camps; some with marketplace layer.
- **Business model:** SaaS per-stable subscription + payment processing cut.
- **Standout:** 24/7 booking, after-hours conversions, multi-instructor scheduling.
- **Region:** Global.

### Horses Middle East (UAE) — *cautionary tale, not a benchmark*
- **What it does:** Pan-GCC marketplace covering horses + tack + equine services.
- **Business model:** Marketplace + classifieds. Launched summer 2020 by a solo Italian founder (Federica D'Andrea); covered once by Gulf News at launch.
- **Reality check (verified June 2026):** Site is effectively abandoned. Similarweb shows ~1,200 monthly visits (May 2026, −59% MoM); newest listings date to Feb 2024; broken placeholder images throughout; no funding announced. An earlier draft of this report cited "100,000 users within months" — that figure could not be sourced and appears to have been fabricated. The only published milestone is "1k Instagram followers in 6 months."
- **Lesson for Indusequine:** Same product shape (horses + tack + services) was attempted in a comparable underserved region and *did not work*. Likely reasons: GCC equestrian buyers transact via personal networks and Instagram DMs, and a solo founder with no capital can't sustain a two-sided marketplace. Implication for India: the shape isn't the moat — the trust layer, federation backing, and operational depth (logistics, vet-check, escrow) are.
- **Region:** GCC.

### EquiRatings (Ireland)
- **What it does:** Data + ratings + KPIs across eventing and show jumping; partner to US Equestrian and other federations.
- **Business model:** B2B SaaS to federations, teams, media; high-performance dashboards; **performance-driven horse-sales search** across ~8,000 active sport horses.
- **Standout:** Performance data layered on top of sale listings — buyers filter horses by Elo, win-clear rate, zone analysis. A premium differentiator for the top end of the Indian market (polo, eventing).
- **Region:** Ireland / global federations.

## 5. Auction / classifieds (live + online)

### Goresbridge Horse Sales (Ireland)
- **What it does:** Europe's largest sport-horse and bloodstock auction house; ~2,000 horses across a 10-day September sale + the elite "Go for Gold" young event horse sale in November.
- **Business model:** Vendor commission + buyer premium; in-person + online bidding.
- **Standout:** **Online viewing gallery available a week ahead of sale + live online bidding** (goresbridgeonlineauctions.com). Curated "elite" sub-sale as a premium brand tier.
- **Region:** Ireland.

### PferdeAuktion / Elite Horses Auction (Germany / EU)
- **What it does:** Online sport-horse auctions, often breed-society linked (Holsteiner, Hanoverian, Oldenburg verbands run their own).
- **Business model:** Vendor commission + buyer premium; sometimes free entry for buyers.
- **Standout:** **Breed-society-run elite auctions** — the model for an India equivalent run jointly with Marwari Horse Society, Kathiawari breeders, or polo pony breeders.
- **Region:** Germany / EU.

## 6. Subscription / supplements / feed

### SmartPak AutoShip — see Section 1.
- The standout reference. Pre-measured monthly delivery + 10% loyalty discount on own-brand tack as the lock-in.

### Chewy (USA, parent post-2025)
- **What it does:** Pet e-com giant now adding equine via SmartEquine acquisition.
- **Standout:** Autoship at scale, deep margin on private label, vet telehealth (Connect with a Vet) bundled with subscription. A model for what a horse-care subscription could look like in India once a critical mass of urban stables exists.
- **Region:** USA.

## 7. Content + community + commerce hybrids

### Horse & Hound Shop (UK)
- **What it does:** 140-year-old equestrian publication with a curated shop and a strong horses-for-sale classifieds rail.
- **Business model:** Ads + subscription + affiliate + classifieds + own shop.
- **Standout:** Editorial-led commerce — gift guides, "best rugs of 2026," buyer's guides driving affiliate. Trust-of-the-masthead is the moat.
- **Region:** UK.

### NoëlleFloyd / NF+ (Global)
- **What it does:** Education-first equestrian platform — masterclasses, structured learning paths, guided rides, community.
- **Business model:** NF+ subscription + commerce + brand sponsorships.
- **Standout:** **"Horse-first" editorial brand → subscription education product (NF+).** Built one of the first equestrian Masterclass-style platforms. The model for an Indusequine "Academy" tier featuring Indian Olympians (Fouaad Mirza, etc.).
- **Region:** Global, US-led.

### EquiSearch (USA)
- **What it does:** Editorial portal with discipline guides, breed encyclopedias.
- **Standout:** Long-tail SEO content as the top-of-funnel for an aging equestrian audience.
- **Region:** USA.

## 8. Booking / experiences

Covered above (Hopoti, Roverd, Anolla, Bookeo, Equclub, SuperSaaS). The dominant pattern is SaaS-for-stables that doubles as a consumer marketplace once enough stables onboard. For India, where most stables still take WhatsApp/phone bookings, **giving away the SaaS to win supply, then monetising consumer demand** is the obvious wedge.

## 9. India and India-adjacent

### Tack Shop India (tackshop.in)
- **What it does:** Curated equestrian retailer with international brands + indigenous tack + own brand "Equenatural."
- **Business model:** Direct retail e-com.
- **Standout:** Currently the most credible India-native specialist; relies heavily on personal trust (founders Saravanan & Vinaya) rather than category depth or platform features.
- **Region:** India.

### Jannat International (India)
- **What it does:** Kanpur-region leather workshop supplying saddles, bridles, horse tack globally.
- **Business model:** Manufacturer / wholesale exporter.
- **Standout:** Indian leather supply chain — relevant as a private-label partner for an Indusequine own-brand line, much like Horze did.
- **Region:** India (export-led).

### Decathlon Fouganza India
- Already on decathlon.in and Flipkart. Owns the entry-level price point. Indusequine should position above it on curation, advice, and discipline expertise — not compete on price for helmets and gloves.

### Equestrian Federation of India (EFI)
- Governing body for Show Jumping, Dressage, Eventing, Endurance, Tent Pegging since 1967. Affiliated to FEI.
- **Opportunity:** Federation-backed classifieds and "EFI-approved" services accreditation — the HorseQuest × British Eventing / BHS Approved Centres playbook in India.

### Horses Middle East
- Closest *structural* analogue (horses + tack + services), but a cautionary tale rather than a benchmark — launched 2020, ~1.2k monthly visits today, listings stale since early 2024. Tells us the marketplace *shape* alone doesn't carry; what's missing is a trust layer, federation backing, and operational depth (logistics, escrow, vet-check). Worth studying for what to avoid.

### Asia
- Japan and China are dominated by racing — JRA in Japan is closed/centralised; China has scattered tack importers but no consumer marketplace of note. Indusequine could plausibly extend to Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, and the GCC South-Asian rider diaspora over time.

## Ideas worth stealing for Indusequine

1. **Vet-check filter on every horse listing** — ehorses.com style. Sellers upload x-rays + pre-purchase vet certificate; buyers filter to "vet-check available" listings. This single feature is the highest-trust differentiator for a market like India where horse-trading is notoriously opaque.

2. **Free listings under a price threshold, paid above** — HorseDeals AUS model. Free for horses under ₹2 lakh, paid premium tiers above. Lowers the barrier so the catalogue fills out, monetises serious listings.

3. **PRO Business subscription for breeders and dealers** — HorseClicks. ₹X/month, includes N free listings, boost credits, and a seller-analytics dashboard with week-over-week views and enquiry counts. Targets Marwari breeders, polo pony dealers, riding-school owners selling off-the-track horses.

4. **Federation-partnership trust layer** — HorseQuest × British Eventing, BHS Approved Centres. Pursue official partnership with EFI, the Amateur Riders' Club Mumbai, Delhi Riding Club, Indian Polo Association. "EFI-listed coach," "EFI-approved riding school" badges on the directory.

5. **Discipline-specific verticals** — Sport Horse Nation. Indian disciplines have very different audiences: polo, tent-pegging, dressage/show-jumping, racing retirees, endurance. Each gets its own filtered sub-site under one platform.

6. **Video + multi-photo as a first-class listing object** — ehorses, Equirodi. Listings without video shouldn't get indexed in search. Add a "verified video" badge for listings where Indusequine has independently watched the horse move.

7. **Pre-measured supplement subscription** — SmartPak SmartPaks. Once Indusequine has supply, "Monthly Horse Box" — joint, gut, hoof supplements pre-portioned per horse, auto-ship monthly. Bundle with 10% off own-brand tack to drive lock-in.

8. **Blanket / saddle / bit fit wizard** — Dover Saddlery. Guided questionnaires that recommend products — high conversion, high trust, low return rate. Build one for saddle fit using local horse breed proportions (Marwari withers are notably different).

9. **Services + goods + horses in a single marketplace** — one search bar, one onboarding, one trust layer. (Horses Middle East tried this shape and failed; the takeaway is that unification is necessary but not sufficient — pair it with the trust layer in #1, #4, and #10.)

10. **Consignment for used tack** — Equine Exchange model. Indusequine receives the saddle in Bangalore/Delhi, authenticates and inspects, ships and takes 30% commission. Solves the trust problem that kills used-tack marketplaces in India.

11. **Stable management SaaS, free to acquire supply** — Hopoti / Roverd. Give riding schools and livery yards a free lesson-booking and invoicing tool; in return Indusequine becomes the consumer-facing booking layer and takes a 10% transaction fee.

12. **EquiRatings-style performance data on premium horse listings** — for the very top end (Asian Games, polo handicaps, eventing). Buyers filter sport horses by competition results, win rates, and ratings, not just price.

13. **Curated editorial + masterclass tier** — NoëlleFloyd / Horse & Hound. NF+ for India: monthly masterclasses from Fouaad Mirza, leading polo players, and farriers; gift guides; "best rain rugs for Bangalore monsoon" buyer's guides for SEO and trust.

14. **Breed-society partnership auctions** — PferdeAuktion model. Run an annual "Indusequine Elite Sale" jointly with Marwari Horse Society or with leading polo pony breeders. Online viewing gallery a week before, live online bidding on sale day. Becomes the brand-making event.

15. **Mobile-first second-hand tack app** — TackTack model. India is mobile-native; the desktop classifieds UX won't work. Camera-first listing flow, in-app chat, UPI checkout, India Post / Delhivery shipping integration.

16. **Jobs board for grooms, syces, instructors** — Yard and Groom. The Indian groom market is entirely informal. A first-mover formal jobs board with role-granular categories (competition groom, polo groom, stable manager) becomes essential infrastructure and a permanent moat.

17. **Circular-economy positioning** — Equirodi. Lean into the resale narrative. A premium platform that *also* gives a second life to expensive saddles is more palatable to first-time buyers nervous about cost.

18. **EFI-approved accreditation as a paid product** — BHS model. Stables and coaches pay an annual fee to be verified and listed; the verification itself is the product. Aligns incentives — Indusequine has reason to actually check standards, and consumers get a real trust signal.
