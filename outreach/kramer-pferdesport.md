# Krämer Pferdesport — supplier approach

**Status: draft, deliberately unsent.** Settled 2026-10-05: this goes out once
Indusequine is open and live, not before. See the blocker at the bottom, which
is the reason.

No contact there yet. It wants a senior person in export or wholesale, who
will check anything the email claims.

---

**Subject:** Krämer in India

Dear [Name],

Indusequine is India's first dedicated equestrian marketplace. We carry 866
products from 51 brands — Waldhausen, Eskadron, Kingsland, Equithème and
Samshield among them — so a good deal of what we stock will be familiar to you.

I'm writing to ask whether Krämer would consider India.

The position here is odd enough to be worth a minute of your time. India has
nine racecourses, around a hundred thoroughbred stud farms, thirty-five polo
clubs and 282 riding clubs affiliated to the national federation. It has no
equestrian retailer of any scale. Last year the country imported about three
million dollars of saddlery while exporting two hundred million. India makes
tack for the world and cannot buy it.

Our customers are the sport riders and the polo clubs rather than the racing
yards — that is the end of the market that wants what you sell. It is also the
end that is growing. India won team dressage gold at the 2023 Asian Games, its
first equestrian gold since 1982, and new riding schools keep opening in Delhi,
Mumbai and Bangalore.

We would handle everything local: listing, customers, enquiries, delivery.
There would be nothing for you to build.

We are early and small, and I would rather say that than oversell it. But the
market is unserved, and being first here costs you very little.

Would you have twenty minutes?

Best regards,
[Your name]
Founder, Indusequine

---

## Why it reads this way

**Racing and polo are context, not the offer.** They establish that India has a
lot of horses. "Our customers are the sport riders and the polo clubs rather
than the racing yards" does real work: it says we know which part of the market
buys German tack, and stops them picturing breeches sold to a Mumbai training
yard.

**Every number is checkable**, which is the point. Nine racecourses, ~100 stud
farms, 33–35 IPA clubs, 282 EFI clubs, the import and export figures, the 2023
gold. Nothing from a market-research PDF, no invented CAGR. The workings are in
`research/market-by-country.md`.

**"India makes tack for the world and cannot buy it"** is the line to keep
whatever else changes. True, surprising, and the whole thesis in nine words.

**"Biggest growing market" was cut.** A $3M import market cannot carry that
sentence and their trade desk would know within a minute.
